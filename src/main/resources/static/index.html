How SecondServe works

Three simple steps to help surplus food reach the right people.

1

Share Surplus Food

Donors list available food, quantities, expiry details, and pickup information.

2

Reserve What You Need

Recipients discover available listings and request suitable quantities.

3

Coordinate Pickup

Donors and recipients coordinate collection while following food safety guidance.
